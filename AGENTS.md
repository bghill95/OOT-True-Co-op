## Agent status records

`docs/agent-status/` is this repository's canonical engineering record, kept to the Navideus Agent Reporting Standard v1.1 (https://github.com/bghill95/navideus-reporting-standard); its `README.md` explains the files and the states.

1. Read `docs/agent-status/STATUS.md` and `ROADMAP.md` before significant work.
2. Inspect the architectural decisions in `DECISIONS.md` that bear on the task.
3. Update the implementation status in `STATUS.md` after a meaningful change.
4. Record actual verification results in `VERIFICATION.md`, and write Not Run for a check that did not run.
5. Add a significant decision to `DECISIONS.md` when it is made, and mark any decision it replaces as superseded.
6. Preserve the open blockers and the next concrete engineering action in `STATUS.md`, and keep them current.
7. Skip trivial or redundant updates, because the goal is accurate continuity, not paperwork.

Merged is not deployed and not released.
